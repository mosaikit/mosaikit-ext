acceptance:
  - An extension declares through a contract of ext-catalog (MK-134) a kind of file, recognized by name, media type or content, and the file manager shows the files of that kind with its icon and its preview element.
  - The extension adds actions to the files of its kind, shown in the list and in the detail of the file, and the file manager works the same without it.
  - The extension is told when a file of its kind is stored, replaced, moved, deleted or shared differently, with the file and its content readable on the server, and may record on the file a state it shows (such as loaded or failed).
  - Removing the extension leaves its files as plain files, with their content, and installing it again recognizes them.
---

A file manager shared by products cannot know every format a product understands. An extension of a
product recognizes its own kinds (a table, an image, a dataset), shows them, adds what can be done
with them and reacts when they change, through a contract of `ext-catalog`; the files stay plain files
of the organization, so nothing is lost when that extension is absent.
