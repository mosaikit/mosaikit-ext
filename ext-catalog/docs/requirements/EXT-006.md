title: Documents in the files are linked to resources of other extensions
area: files
priority: P2
status: in-progress
depends: [EXT-001, MK-041, MK-054, MK-055, MK-093]
estimate: [5, 8]
acceptance:
  - A document uploaded to the files is found by the global search and shown with its own sheet.
  - A document linked to a resource of another extension (for example a connection or a dashboard) is listed on the sheet of that resource, and the resource on the sheet of the document.
  - A document shared with a group is downloaded by its members through a signed link that expires; a person outside the group neither finds nor downloads it.
  - When the extension of a linked resource is not installed, the link shows the placeholder of the platform (MK-135) instead of an error.
---

Reports, official acts, scanned papers: files that stand next to the resources they refer to. The
file manager keeps them as its own items and lets other extensions link them to their resources, through
a contract the file manager offers (MK-134). A link to a resource of an absent extension stays and is
shown as unavailable.
