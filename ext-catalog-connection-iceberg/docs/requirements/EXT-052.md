---
id: EXT-052
title: Open table catalogs (Iceberg REST and equivalents) list their tables
area: connections
priority: P2
status: proposed
depends: [EXT-010]
estimate: [6, 9]
acceptance:
  - An Iceberg REST catalog registered as a connection lists its namespaces and tables, each with schema, partitions and snapshots.
  - Choosing a table from the list gives a reference other extensions read, without typing its path.
  - A catalog that refuses the credentials shows the error it returned instead of an empty list.
---

Iceberg REST and its equivalents (Unity, Glue, Hive Metastore) are declared once as a connection
(`ext-catalog-connection-iceberg`), and their tables appear as its parts. On a lakehouse with thousands
of tables, choosing from a list instead of pasting a path makes all the difference.
