---
id: EXT-100
title: A process node delegates work to an external compute engine and waits for its outcome
area: processes
priority: P1
status: proposed
depends: [EXT-094]
estimate: [10, 15]
acceptance:
  - A delegate node submits a job to an external engine (for example a Spark or warehouse job) with its parameters and the run waits until the job ends.
  - The run log shows the external job id, its log excerpt, its outcome and its reported cost.
  - A server restart while the external job runs resumes polling and completes the run.
---

A node submits the work to the organization's engine and waits for the outcome, with parameters, log
and cost reported in the run: external job, polling, resume at the end. When volume exceeds what the
executor can do, delegate to whoever owns the cluster.
