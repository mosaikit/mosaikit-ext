  - The process editor shows the nodes grouped by category, rendered as cards with a title bar coloured by category.

  - Selecting a node opens a guide panel with its description and its typed inputs and outputs.
  - The editor supports collapsible groups, automatic layout, frames and annotations, and they are kept when the process is saved and reopened.
---

`ext-processes` is the visual process editor, based on LiteGraph: a palette of nodes by category,
rendered as cards with a coloured title bar, collapsible groups, automatic layout, frames and annotations,
links coloured by data type, and a guide panel per node. Nodes for a domain are contributed by other
extensions through the node contract (EXT-065).
