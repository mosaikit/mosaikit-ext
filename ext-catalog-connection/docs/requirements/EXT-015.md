acceptance:
  - Creating any kind of connection records the person who created it, and the card shows that owner.
  - The creation and edit forms of every connector have a description field right under the name, and saving it empty clears it.
  - Under the name, a card shows the description, or the address of the source when there is no description.
---

Every connection has an owner and a description, whatever its connector. Under the name the card
shows one thing with one rule: the description, or, when missing, the address of the source.

## History

- 2026-10-14: moved here from GP-296, a requirement of a product that composes these extensions; rewritten without what was specific to that product.
- 2026-10-15: in progress.
- 2026-10-15: done: each connection is a resource of the kernel (MK-054), whose owner is the person
  who creates it, with any connector; the forms of creation and edit have a description right under
  the name, cleared when saved empty; the card shows the description or the address of the source,
  which the connector tells (`Connector.address`, a default method of version 1 of the contract) or
  the framework takes from the usual settings, without user and password. Tests: `tests/list.spec.ts`,
  `AddressesTest`.
