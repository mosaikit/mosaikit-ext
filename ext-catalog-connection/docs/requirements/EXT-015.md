  - Creating any kind of connection records the person who created it, and the card shows that owner.

  - Under the name, a card shows the description, or the address of the source when there is no description.

Every connection has an owner and a description, whatever its connector. Under the name the card
shows one thing with one rule: the description, or, when missing, the address of the source.
