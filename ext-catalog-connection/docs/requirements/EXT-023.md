estimate: [12, 18]
acceptance:
  - Choosing a category lists the connections of that category; choosing a connection shows its contents and actions in the detail.
  - The command New opens the creation form in the detail, already set to the category being viewed, and testing the connection shows the contents the server read.
  - Connecting the same curated entry twice is refused with a message naming the existing connection.
---

Categories on the left, connections in the middle, contents on the right with edits and shortcuts.
The detail column does two jobs: detail when a connection is chosen, form when New is pressed; the
connection test produces the preview, so creating and reading share one panel. The categories live in
the list pane of the shell. Connections created from a curated entry contributed by a connector record
their origin, so one entry is connected once.
