acceptance:
  - The first step lists every installed connector type with its logo, grouped by category.
  - On the second step a successful connection test shows a preview of the content of the source, and a failed one shows the error returned by the source.
  - On the third step the name is proposed, the type is fixed, and tags can be added; saving closes the panel and the new connection appears in the list.
  - A connector reserved to a paid extension shows the refusal instead of failing silently.
---

One path for every kind of source. Step 1, **what it is**: the connector types with their logos,
grouped by category. Step 2, **how to connect**: the connection form the connector declares, where the
connection test is a preview of the content rather than a green tick. Step 3, **how it is named and
found**: name, description, thumbnail, the fixed type and free tags.
