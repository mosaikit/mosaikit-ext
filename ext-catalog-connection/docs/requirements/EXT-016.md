title: The detail of a connection opens as a panel over the list, with its actions fixed at the bottom
area: connections
priority: P1
status: in-progress
depends: [EXT-011]
estimate: [1, 2]
acceptance:
  - Clicking a card opens the detail panel on the right edge, over the list, without moving the other cards.
  - The facts and tags of the connection stay in place while the list of its parts scrolls; the actions stay at the bottom.
  - Clicking the same card again or outside the panel closes it; clicking another card shows that card's detail.
  - The open card is visibly marked as selected and announced with aria-current.
---

The detail overlays the list instead of sitting beside it, so looking at one connection does not
reflow the others: attached to the right edge, full height, header, body, fixed footer. Only the list of
parts scrolls. The actions (create a resource from a part, edit, test, delete) stay at the bottom.
