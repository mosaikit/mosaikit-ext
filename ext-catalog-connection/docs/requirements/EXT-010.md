`ext-catalog-connection` is the framework of connections to data sources: the list, the creation
path, the detail, the picker other extensions open, the encrypted credentials and the content model. It
knows no protocol. Each kind of source is a connector, an extension of its own named
`ext-catalog-connection-<source>`, which declares itself through a contract of the framework (MK-134); a
product installs the connectors it needs, and its own specific connectors next to the generic ones of
this repository. Credentials of every connection are kept by the secrets service of the platform and are
never returned by the API.
