acceptance:
  - Every connection shows its type, derived from its connector, which cannot be removed.
  - Every connection shows a category from the taxonomy of the connectors, so that no connection is without a tag.
  - Free tags written on a connection are stored lowercase without duplicates and appear in the Tags filter.
  - Filtering by a type and a tag together returns the intersection; two tags of the same list return the union.
---

Three kinds of label that look different: the **type** (a fact, derived from the connector and not
removable), the **category** (from the taxonomy the connectors contribute, derived from the type) and
the **free tag** written by a person through the generic tags of the platform (MK-097). Types and tags
are two separate filter columns, since "what kind is it" and "what is it about" are different questions.
