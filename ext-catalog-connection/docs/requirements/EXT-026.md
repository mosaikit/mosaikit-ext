acceptance:
  - The dashboard and process editors open the same picker, with filters, connections as cards and the parts of the chosen connection, and another extension can open it through the contract of the framework (MK-134).
  - In each context the Type filter and the parts offered are limited to the uses that editor declared.
  - The picker supports single and multiple choice, and returns a reference to the connection and its part, so that a later change of the source address is followed by the users of the reference.
  - The filter column can be widened by dragging or with the keyboard.
---

The question "what data do I put here" has one answer everywhere: one picker, offered by the
framework to the editors of the other extensions, with a **context** that filters types and parts by the
uses declared. It is the connections view itself, not a copy of it, and it returns a reference, not a
copied address.

## History

- 2026-10-14: moved here from GP-315, a requirement of a product that composes these extensions; rewritten without what was specific to that product.
- 2026-10-15: in progress.
- 2026-10-15: the picker is the view of the connections in a dialog, the element
  `mosaikit-ext-catalog-connection-picker` (version 1 of its contract), which the framework defines
  for the extensions that run as modules: the opening extension names itself, and optionally some of
  its uses of `catalog.connection.use`; the Type filter keeps the types whose connectors hold a kind
  those uses take (the new default method `Connector.kinds`, or the kinds usual for the category),
  and only the pieces they take can be chosen, one or several. It returns references by id
  (the connection as a resource of the platform, the piece with the ids of its containers), which
  `POST /api/v1/ext/connections/references/resolve` gives as they are now, address included. The
  column of filters, in the app and in the picker, is the resizable pane of the UI kit (MK-105), or a
  handle of the extension with the same behaviour where the shell does not define it
  (mosaikit/mosaikit#173). Tests `picker.spec.ts` (with an editor of the tests,
  `tests/fixtures/editor`) and `KindsTest` cover criteria 2 to 4, and criterion 1 through that
  editor; it stays in progress until the editors of dashboards and processes (M3) open the picker
  themselves. An editor isolated in a frame cannot open it yet: the element is defined in the page
  of the shell, not in the frame.
