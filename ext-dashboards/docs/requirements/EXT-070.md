`ext-dashboards` offers the base widget catalog: charts, indicators, tables, content, and the
operations-room widgets (indices, linear and radial indicators, clock, weather, news ticker, alert feed
and the smart grid). Widgets of other kinds (a map, for example) are contributed by other extensions
through the widget contract (EXT-065).
