---
id: EXT-080
title: A widget of an extension that is not installed is shown as a placeholder
area: dashboards
priority: P1
status: planned
depends: [EXT-070]
estimate: [1, 2]
acceptance:
  - A dashboard holding a widget contributed by an extension that is not installed opens, shows the other widgets, and shows the placeholder of the platform (MK-135) in the place of the missing one, naming the extension.
  - Saving such a dashboard keeps the definition of the missing widget unchanged.
  - When the extension is installed again the widget renders with its settings.
---

Dashboards hold widgets contributed by other extensions, which an installation may not have. The
dashboard degrades instead of failing: the definition stays, and the place shows what is missing.
