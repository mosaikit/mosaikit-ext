title: One source picker for the editors of every extension
area: connections
priority: P1
status: planned
depends: [EXT-011, EXT-012]
estimate: [5, 8]
acceptance:
  - The dashboard and process editors open the same picker, with filters, connections as cards and the parts of the chosen connection, and another extension can open it through the contract of the framework (MK-134).
  - In each context the Type filter and the parts offered are limited to the uses that editor declared.
  - The picker supports single and multiple choice, and returns a reference to the connection and its part, so that a later change of the source address is followed by the users of the reference.
  - The filter column can be widened by dragging or with the keyboard.
---

The question "what data do I put here" has one answer everywhere: one picker, offered by the
framework to the editors of the other extensions, with a **context** that filters types and parts by the
uses declared. It is the connections view itself, not a copy of it, and it returns a reference, not a
copied address.
