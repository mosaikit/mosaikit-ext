id: EXT-029
title: Saved queries become materialized extracts with a cadence, and panels show their freshness
area: connections
priority: P1
status: proposed
depends: [EXT-028]
estimate: [10, 15]
acceptance:
  - A saved query set to materialize hourly is executed on the source once an hour, whatever the number of open dashboards that read it.
  - Every panel reading an extract shows the time the extract was last refreshed.
  - The policy of a connection (query in place, materialize with cadence, extract once) is visible on the connection.
---

A saved query becomes a materialized table or a columnar extract with its own cadence, and panels read
that. On a pay-per-use warehouse a dashboard left open on a wall screen otherwise queries the source on
every look. Freshness is stated on the panel.
