area: common

depends: [MK-033, MK-055]
estimate: [4, 6]
acceptance:
  - Two people with the same dashboard open each see the other's avatar in its header.
  - The share action appears in the header only to a person who can manage the resource.
  - Share opens the sharing dialog of that resource, the same one the list menu opens.
  - The header is a component other extensions use for their own resources.
---

The header of an open resource answers together: what am I looking at, who else is here, and what can
I do with it. Presence avatars, sharing and the editor/view mode sit in one group on the right.
