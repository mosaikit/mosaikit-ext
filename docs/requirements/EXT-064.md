title: The lineage of every resource, from connection to report, in one clickable graph
area: common
priority: P2
status: proposed
depends: [MK-054]
estimate: [8, 12]
acceptance:
  - The lineage panel of a dashboard shows the connections it reads and the processes that produce its data.
  - Clicking a node of the graph opens that resource; a node of an extension that is not installed shows the placeholder of the platform (MK-135).
  - The lineage panel of a connection lists the resources that depend on it, so that the impact of changing it is visible before changing it.
  - An extension declares the references of its resources through a contract, so its resources appear in the graph.
---

Whoever reaches a resource does not know where it comes from, and whoever changes a connection does not
know what will break. From any resource one goes up and down the chain in one panel with a clickable
graph, built from the references the extensions declare.
