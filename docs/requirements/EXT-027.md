---
id: EXT-027
title: Connection cards are compact and show the logo of their type
area: connections
priority: P2
status: proposed
depends: [EXT-011, EXT-014]
estimate: [4, 7]
acceptance:
  - In the grid a card has no image; it shows name, description, type with its logo, tags, and status and date in the footer.
  - The logo of a type is the same in the Type filter, on the card and on the first step of New connection.
  - When there are two filter columns they appear as two tabs, each using the full height of the side bar.
  - The selection box is visible on cards in both light and dark themes, and logos remain readable on dark backgrounds.
---

Small changes that lower the cost of reading a list of hundreds of connections. The type is shown by
the logo its connector declares, the same in the filter, on the card and in New connection.
