---
id: EXT-060
title: Resource cards of the common extensions show the same signals in the same places
area: common
priority: P1
status: proposed
depends: [MK-033, MK-055]
estimate: [3, 5]
acceptance:
  - Dashboard, process, file and connection cards show last activity, owner and sharing state in the same positions.
  - The last activity of a card is the time of the last change, not the creation time.
  - A person who has a dashboard open appears on its card in the list of another person, without each card opening its own connection.
  - A missing signal leaves no gap, and the selection checkbox stays clickable in its corner.
  - The card model is offered to other extensions, so a product's own resources read the same.
---

Cards must read at a glance with the same signals in the same places: thumbnail, presence, alert
state, sharing and last activity. The signals are declared fields of one card model, not markup each list
builds by itself. Presence (MK-033) comes as a snapshot inside the list answer, limited to the rows shown.
