acceptance:
  - A dashboard duplicated from its card appears as a new card with the same widgets and the current person as author.
  - Filtering by a tag shows only the resources with that tag.
  - A deleted process disappears from the list and its link answers not found.
  - The global search of the platform finds dashboards and processes by name.
---

Dashboards and processes are resources of the organization. Each has a list page with cards, search
and tags; each resource is created, saved, opened, duplicated and deleted, and shows its author, date and
thumbnail. The global search of the platform (MK-040) finds them.
