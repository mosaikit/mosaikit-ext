acceptance:
  - Sharing a folder with a group gives its members access to the folder and to everything inside it, including items added later.
  - A file shared with one person outside the folder's sharing is visible to that person in Shared with me, and the rest of the folder is not.
  - The sharing dialog is the one of the platform (MK-055), with the levels read, edit and manage, and the folder shows who has access and through which share.
  - 'Removing a share takes effect at the next request of the person: the folder disappears from their list and its files can no longer be downloaded.'
---

Sharing works like in team file spaces: a share on a folder is inherited by its content, a file can
be shared on its own, and every person has a Shared with me view next to the files of the organization.
Levels and subjects are those of the sharing model of the platform (MK-055); the extension adds the
inheritance from a folder to its content.
