title: Report templates turn process outputs into designed PDF documents
area: processes
priority: P1
status: proposed
depends: [EXT-092]
estimate: [12, 18]
acceptance:
  - Running a process with a report template attached produces a PDF with the table and chart of its outputs.
  - A scheduled process produces one PDF per execution, listed with the execution.
  - A block contributed by another extension (for example an image of a resource of that extension) is rendered in the PDF, and is left out with a note when that extension is not installed.
---

A report definition is bound to the outputs of a process: a designed document with tables, charts and
images, produced as PDF on demand, on the schedule of the process, or at the end of an execution. Blocks
are bound to named outputs; other extensions contribute block kinds.
