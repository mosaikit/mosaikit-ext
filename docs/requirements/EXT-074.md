---
id: EXT-074
title: Attribute and time filters cross between dashboard widgets
area: dashboards
priority: P1
status: planned
depends: [EXT-070]
estimate: [5, 8]
acceptance:
  - Selecting a category in a chart filters the other widgets bound to the same data.
  - Changing the time range of the dashboard filters every widget that declares a time field.
  - A widget contributed by another extension receives and emits filters through the filter contract of the dashboards, so it takes part in cross filtering (a map widget, for example, adds the area in view).
---

Filters propagate both ways between widgets. The dashboard owns the filter state; widgets declare the
fields they filter on and the filters they emit, through a contract other extensions implement for their
widgets (MK-134).
