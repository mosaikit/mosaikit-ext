---
id: EXT-020
title: A resource of another extension is created directly from a chosen piece of a connection
area: connections
priority: P1
status: planned
depends: [EXT-019]
estimate: [3, 5]
acceptance:
  - Choosing a table and "new dashboard" opens a dashboard with a sample widget bound to that table, and the widget shows its data.
  - Choosing a piece and "new process" opens the process editor with an input node already set to it.
  - A use contributed by an extension that is not installed is not offered.
  - With no piece chosen the commands are disabled and explain why.
---

From the detail of a connection, once a piece is chosen, people create directly what they want to do
with it. Each consuming extension contributes its command through a contract (MK-134) and receives a
reference to the piece, not a copy of its address. Without a chosen piece the commands stay disabled.
