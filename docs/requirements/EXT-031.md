title: Queries on connections have a byte budget per organization and connection, and a log
area: connections
priority: P1
status: proposed
depends: [EXT-028, MK-076]
estimate: [8, 12]
acceptance:
  - Where the source exposes a dry run, the editor shows the estimated bytes before execution.
  - A query whose estimate exceeds the remaining budget of the organization or the connection is refused with a message naming the limit, and is not sent to the source.
  - The log of the organization lists every query with who ran it, when, its duration, bytes read and rows returned.
---

An estimate of the bytes before execution where the source exposes it, a limit per organization and
per connection through the quota model of the platform (MK-076), interruption with the reason, and a log.
