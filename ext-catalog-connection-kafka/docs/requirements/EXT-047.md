  - With `ext-catalog-connection-kafka` installed, New connection offers a Kafka cluster with its connection form.
  - The connection test returns either a preview of the content or the error of the remote system.
  - The connector lists topics, shows a sample of records and relays them through the server.
  - Credentials of the connection are stored encrypted and never returned by the API.
---

The connector `ext-catalog-connection-kafka` (manifest id
`io.github.mosaikit.ext.catalog.connection.kafka`) adds a Kafka cluster to the connection framework (EXT-010).
It is an extension of its own: an installation that does not need it does not carry it, and connections
made with it show as unavailable when it is removed (MK-135).
