  - With `ext-catalog-connection-websocket` installed, New connection offers a WebSocket endpoint with its connection form.

  - The connector shows a sample of messages and relays them to the browser through the server.
  - Credentials of the connection are stored encrypted and never returned by the API.

The connector `ext-catalog-connection-websocket` (manifest id
`io.github.mosaikit.ext.catalog.connection.websocket`) adds a WebSocket endpoint to the connection framework (EXT-010).
It is an extension of its own: an installation that does not need it does not carry it, and connections
made with it show as unavailable when it is removed (MK-135).
